# Ratna Aura — Delete your account and data

**Publisher:** Raksha, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India · **Contact:** velqorasupport99@gmail.com

You can delete your Ratna Aura account and all its data at any time, in the app or by e-mail.

## Option 1 — in the app (immediate)
1. Open Ratna Aura and sign in.
2. Tap the **Settings** (gear) icon at the top of the home screen.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete my account and data**.
4. Tap **Yes, delete everything** to confirm.

## Option 2 — by e-mail (if you no longer have the app)
Write to **velqorasupport99@gmail.com** with the subject **"Delete my account"**, from the e-mail address of your
Ratna Aura account (or tell us the mobile number you signed in with). We may ask you to confirm that the account is
yours. We delete the account within **7 days** and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted
- Your account, sign-in details, name, language and consent records.
- All saved people and their birth details, kundlis, chats with Ask AI and the AI astrologers, palm readings, reported
  answers, credits, unlocked tools and history.

Data is removed from our database **immediately**; backups are overwritten within **30 days**. Unused credits are
lost and cannot be refunded.

## What may be kept
Records of purchases (order number, date, amount) may be kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires
(up to 8 years). They do not include your birth details, chats or readings.

**Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription.** Cancel it first in Google Play → Payments &
subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ratna Aura → Cancel.
