**Ratna Aura** brings Vedic astrology and numerology to you in **English and 30 Indian languages** — Hindi, Bengali,

planetary positions.

📅 **Panchang** — tithi, nakshatra, yoga, karana with exact times, sunrise and sunset, Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda, Gulika,
Abhijit muhurta and the Hindu month, for any city.

🔢 **Numerology** — Mulank, Bhagyank, name number (Chaldean & Pythagorean), Lo Shu grid, name correction, mobile and
vehicle number vibrations, and compatibility.

🤖 **Ask AI** — ask questions about your chart and numbers and get thoughtful answers in your language. Your first
3 questions are free.

🧘 **Talk to AI astrologers** — chat with five AI guides, each with a specialty: Vedic astrology, numerology, love &
marriage, traditional panchang & muhurta, and career. They are clearly AI — never pretending to be human.

🔒 **Private by design** — birth details and chats are encrypted; delete your account and data any time from Settings.

Readings are for guidance and reflection. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.
For adults (18+). If you are going through a hard time, Tele-MANAS is free and available 24×7: 14416.
