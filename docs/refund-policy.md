# Ratna Aura — Refund & Cancellation Policy

All purchases in Ratna Aura (credit packs and the Premium subscription) are digital and are made through
**Google Play**. This policy explains when you can get a refund and how to cancel. It is part of our Terms of Use.

## 1. Automatic protections (no request needed)
- **AI answer failed:** if an Ask AI question or a paid AI astrologer message cannot be answered because of a
  technical problem, the credit is **returned automatically**.
- **Palm reading:** if the photos do not show a clear palm, or the reading cannot be made, **your payment is kept**
  so you can take new photos and try again without paying twice.
- **Paid tools:** a result you unlocked stays unlocked; you never pay twice for the same person, pair, name,
  number or month.

## 2. Refunds through Google Play
- **Within 48 hours of a purchase**, you can request a refund directly from Google Play
  (play.google.com → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or Google's refund request page).
  Google decides these requests under its own policy.
- **After 48 hours**, write to us (section 5). We will refund, through Google Play, in these cases:
  - you were charged more than once for the same purchase;
  - you paid but the credits or Premium were not added, and we cannot fix it;
  - a paid feature did not work because of a fault on our side that we could not fix within a reasonable time;
  - any other case where Indian consumer law entitles you to a refund.
- **Not refundable:** credits already used; results already unlocked and shown; per-minute AI astrologer sessions
  already started; and change of mind after the result has been delivered — except where the law requires otherwise.
- Unused credits have no cash value and are not exchanged for money, except as required by law.

## 3. Premium subscription — cancelling
- You can cancel **any time** in **Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Ratna Aura → Cancel**,
  or from the app (Premium → Manage subscription). Uninstalling the app does **not** cancel a subscription.
- Cancel at least **24 hours before the renewal date** to avoid the next charge. After you cancel, Premium stays on
  until the end of the period you paid for; we do not give partial refunds for the remaining days, except as Google
  Play or the law provides.
- **Free trial:** cancel before the trial ends and you will not be charged. If you do not cancel, the plan's price is
  charged when the trial ends.
- If a renewal payment fails, Google Play retries for a short grace period, during which Premium stays on. If it
  still fails, Premium ends.

## 4. Deleting your account
Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account and data) permanently deletes any **unused credits** and unlocked
results, and they cannot be restored or refunded. Deleting the account does **not** cancel a Google Play
subscription — please cancel it in Google Play first.

## 5. How to request a refund or raise a complaint
E-mail **velqorasupport99@gmail.com** with:
- the e-mail address or mobile number of your Ratna Aura account;
- the Google Play **order number** (it starts with "GPA.", and is in your Google Play receipt e-mail);
- what went wrong.

We acknowledge your request within **48 hours** and decide within **7 working days**. Approved refunds are made to
your original payment method through Google Play, usually within **5–10 working days**, depending on your bank.
