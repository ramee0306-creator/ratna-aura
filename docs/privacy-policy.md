# Ratna Aura — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [fill in on publishing] · **Publisher:** Raksha · **Contact / Grievance Officer:** velqorasupport99@gmail.com

Ratna Aura ("the app", "we") is a Vedic astrology and numerology app. This policy explains what personal data
we collect, why, who processes it, and the choices you have. It is written to meet India's Digital Personal Data
Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Google Play User Data policy.

> ⚠️ This is a carefully prepared draft, not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before launch.

## 1. Who can use the app
The app is for adults **18 years or older**. We ask every user to confirm this before creating an account. We do
not knowingly collect data from children; if you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will
delete it.

## 2. What we collect

| Data | Why | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number **or** Google e-mail address | To sign you in and protect your account | Supabase (our database provider) |
| Display name, preferred language, consent date and version, 18+ confirmation | To run your account and record your consent | Supabase |
| Names, gender, date, time and place of birth of you and the people you add | To calculate kundlis, matching and numerology | Supabase, **encrypted** with a key only our server holds |
| Your "Ask AI" and persona chat messages and the AI's answers | To answer you and show your conversation history | Supabase, **encrypted** |
| Credits, purchases and credit use | To provide paid features and prevent fraud | Supabase |
| Technical logs (time of request, errors, AI token counts) | To keep the service working and measure costs | Supabase / our server logs, kept up to 30 days |

We do **not** collect your contacts, photos, precise location, or advertising ID, and we do not show ads.
The daily reminder is scheduled **on your phone**; it sends nothing to us.
Voice input (if you use it) is converted to text by your phone's own speech service; we receive only the text.

## 3. AI processing
Answers in "Ask AI", the AI personas and the daily rashifal are written by **Claude**, an AI model from
**Anthropic, PBC**. To answer you, our server sends Anthropic your question, the recent messages of that
conversation, and a summary of the chosen chart (name, birth details, planet positions and numbers). Anthropic
processes this only to produce the answer and, under its commercial terms, does not use it to train its models.
AI answers are for guidance and reflection only and can be wrong. They are not medical, legal or financial advice.

## 4. Who processes your data (our service providers)
- **Supabase** (database, sign-in, server functions), hosted in the Mumbai (ap-south-1) region.
- **Anthropic** (AI answers), servers outside India.
- **Google** (Google sign-in; Google Play for downloads and payments).
- **RevenueCat** (processing Google Play purchases), once paid credits are switched on.
- **SMS provider** (sending one-time passwords), once phone sign-in is switched on.
We do **not** sell your data or share it for advertising.

## 5. How long we keep it
We keep your data while your account exists. When you **delete your account** (Settings → Delete my account and
data), your account, saved people, chats, credits and history are deleted immediately from our database; backups
are overwritten within 30 days. Purchase records may be kept longer where the law requires (e.g. tax records).

## 6. Your rights
You can **see, correct and delete** your saved people in the app, and delete your whole account at any time. Under
the DPDP Act you may also ask us for a summary of your data, correction, erasure, or to withdraw consent, and you
may nominate someone to act for you. Write to **velqorasupport99@gmail.com**; we reply within 30 days. If you are not
satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 7. Security
Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Birth details and chats are additionally encrypted in the
database with AES-256-GCM using a key that never leaves our server. Database rules stop one user from reading
another's data. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as
the law requires.

## 8. Changes
If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
