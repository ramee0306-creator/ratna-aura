This Privacy Policy explains how Ratna Aura ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your
personal data, and the rights you have. It is written for India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
("DPDP Act"), the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules under it, and the Google Play User Data policy.
By creating an account and using the app you give the consent described below; you can withdraw it at any time.

## 1. Who we are
Ratna Aura is a Vedic astrology, numerology and palm-reading app published by **Raksha**, an individual based in
**Mumbai, Maharashtra, India**. For your personal data we are the **Data Fiduciary**: we decide why and how it is
processed. Contact us at **velqorasupport99@gmail.com**.

## 2. Who can use the app
The app is only for adults **18 years or older**. Every user confirms this before an account is created. We do not
knowingly create accounts for children. If you add the birth details of a child (for example, your son or daughter)
to make their kundli, you confirm that you are their parent or lawful guardian and give consent on their behalf.
If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 18, we delete it.

## 3. What we collect, and why

| Personal data | Why we use it | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile number **or** Google e-mail address | To sign you in and protect your account | Supabase (our database provider) |
| Display name, language, 18+ confirmation, consent date and version | To run your account and record your consent | Supabase |
| Names, gender, date, time and place of birth of you and the people you add | To calculate kundlis, matching, panchang and numerology | Supabase, **encrypted** with a key only our server holds |
| Your messages to "Ask AI" and the AI astrologers, and their answers | To answer you and show your conversation history | Supabase, **encrypted** |
| Palm photos you take or choose for a palm reading | Only to make that one reading | **Not stored.** Sent once to our AI provider for the reading, then discarded |
| Your palm readings (the text) | To show you your earlier readings | Supabase, **encrypted** |
| AI answers you report, with your reason and note | To review harmful or wrong answers and improve safety | Supabase, the answer text **encrypted** |
| Credits, purchases, subscriptions, credit use and the tools you unlocked | To provide paid features, prevent fraud, and keep records the law requires | Supabase (tools you unlocked are stored only as a one-way code, not as names or numbers) |
| Technical logs (time of request, errors, AI usage counts) | To keep the service working, secure and affordable | Supabase and server logs, kept up to 30 days |

**We do not collect** your contacts, precise location, other photos or files, call logs, SMS, advertising ID or
browsing history. The camera and your photos are used **only** when you tap to take or choose a palm photo. Voice
input, if you use it, is turned into text by your phone's own speech service; we receive only the text. The daily
reminder is scheduled on your phone and sends nothing to us. We show **no ads** and use **no advertising or
analytics trackers**.

Palm photos are used only to read the lines of your palm. We do not use them, or any other data, to identify you
(no biometric identification) and we do not keep them.

## 4. AI processing
Ask AI, the AI astrologers, palm readings and the daily rashifal are written by **Claude**, an AI model from
**Anthropic, PBC** (United States). To answer you, our server sends Anthropic your question, the recent messages
of that conversation and a summary of the chosen chart (name, birth details, planet positions and numbers); for a
palm reading, the palm photos you chose. Anthropic processes this only to produce the answer and, under its
commercial terms, **does not use it to train its models**. AI answers can be wrong; they are for guidance and
reflection only. You can report any AI answer with the flag next to it.

## 5. Who we share data with
We **never sell** your personal data and never share it for advertising. We share it only with service providers
that process it on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:
- **Supabase** — database, sign-in and server functions, hosted in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1).
- **Anthropic** — AI answers and palm readings (United States).
- **Google** — Google sign-in, and Google Play for app downloads, payments and subscriptions. We never receive your
  card, UPI or bank details; Google Play handles the payment.
- **RevenueCat** — confirms Google Play purchases and subscriptions to our server (only your account id and the
  purchase details).
- **SMS provider** — sends one-time passwords, once phone sign-in is switched on (only your mobile number).

We may also disclose data when Indian law requires it (for example, a valid order of a court or government
authority), or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the app.

## 6. Transfers outside India
Your account data is stored in India. AI requests are processed by Anthropic in the United States, and some Google
and RevenueCat services may process data outside India. These transfers are allowed under the DPDP Act (to
countries not restricted by the Government of India) and are protected by the providers' contracts and encryption
in transit.

## 7. How long we keep data
- While your account exists, we keep the data needed to run it.
- When you **delete your account** (Settings → Delete my account and data), your account, saved people, chats,
  palm readings, credits and history are deleted from our database **immediately**; backups are overwritten within
  **30 days**.
- Palm photos are never stored. Technical logs are kept up to 30 days.
- Records of purchases may be kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.

## 8. How we protect your data
- Birth details, chats, palm readings and reported answers are encrypted with **AES-256-GCM** using a key only our
  server holds; all connections use HTTPS (TLS).
- Strict database rules let each person read only their own data; only our server can make changes.
- Our secret keys are never inside the app or in our source code.
- The app blocks screenshots and screen recording of its screens.
- If a personal data breach happens, we will inform the Data Protection Board of India and the affected users as
  the DPDP Act requires.

## 9. Your rights
Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:
- **Access** a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, and the list of those we share it with;
- **Correct, complete or update** it (you can edit saved people in the app, or write to us);
- **Erase** it (Settings → Delete my account and data, or write to us);
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, as easily as you gave it — by deleting your account or writing to us. This
  does not affect processing done before withdrawal; some features cannot work without the data;
- **Nominate** another person to exercise these rights if you die or become unable to;
- **Grievance redressal** — see section 13.

To use a right, write to **velqorasupport99@gmail.com** from the e-mail or phone linked to your account (we may ask
you to confirm it is you). We reply within **30 days**. If you add other people's details (family, partner), they
can also write to us to have their details removed.
